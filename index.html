Free agent · Local-first

An agent for the meetings, the paperwork, and the words.

IEP Agent is a free assistant that runs on your own computer and helps you build and keep your child's IEP: the about-me picture, the goals, the support team, your notes, and a one-page brief for every meeting. No accounts. Nothing leaves the house unless you say so.

The about-me picture, the goals, the team, the notes. One folder.

The parts of an IEP that actually decide how the year goes. The agent keeps them current and turns them into the two things that matter: a plan page that prints, and a brief for the meeting.

About Me, strengths first

A long-term goal tied to the school's plan, and short-term goals that each carry the concrete strategies behind them.

Notes the moment they happen

Quick notes from the car park, teacher notes by class, all dated. Nothing lost by the time the review comes around.

How the pieces hand over to each other: the plan gets built, the record stays current, and everything lands in the folder you own. Meeting Prep and Export turn it into what you carry into the room.

Carried over from the app

The original app made four promises. All four now live in the agent.

01

Summaries that make sense

Paste in any IEP or functional assessment. It comes back organised, in plain words, with three questions worth asking.

02

Sparky grew up

The app's advisor is now the agent itself: any question about the plan, any time, answered from your own folder. No diagnosis, no verdicts.

03

The learning quest

Sparks, levels and badges for the wins that matter: goals met, notes kept, school days logged. A little fun, never a leash.

04

Accessible and gentle

Built with WCAG in mind: real contrast, real focus states, keyboard-first, and a plan page that prints clean on any school printer.

Fifteen minutes to set up. Two minutes a day after that.

The intake copies what the original IEP Companion app asked, as a conversation.

Tell it about your child

First name, year level, and paste in your existing IEP or assessment if you have one. Skip anything you don't. Nothing is ever invented.

Review the plan together

The agent drafts the about-me picture, goals and support team. You correct it before anything is saved. Your framing wins.

Walk in organised

Before any meeting, ask for a brief: your points, your questions, the goals you are holding to, on one printable page.

Your child's plan is yours

  • The plan folder lives on your computer. Not on a server, not in an account, not with us.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you name a person and say yes, in the moment, every time.
  • Run it on a local model and the whole loop stays offline.
  • Export any time: a printable page, a plain JSON file, a folder you can move to a new laptop.
  • We use a cookieless page counter on this site (OneDollarStats); it sets no cookies and cannot identify you.
