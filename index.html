An ally for the meetings, the paperwork, and the words.

IEP Ally is a free assistant that runs on your own computer and helps you build and keep your child's IEP: the about-me picture, the goals, the support team, your notes, and a one-page brief for every meeting. No accounts. Nothing leaves the house unless you say so.

Download free How it works
Free for everyone. Source-available on GitHub, built on Hermes Agent, runs on your machine or a local model.

The about-me picture, the goals, the team, the notes. One folder.

The parts of an IEP that actually decide how the year goes. The ally keeps them current and turns them into the two things that matter: a plan page that prints, and a brief for the meeting.

About Me, strengths first

Strengths, challenges, what helps learning and how your child communicates best, written in plain words and kept kind.

One big goal, daily wins

A long-term goal tied to the school's plan, and short-term goals that each carry the concrete strategies behind them.

Notes the moment they happen

Quick notes from the car park, teacher notes by class, all dated. Nothing lost by the time the review comes around.

Fifteen minutes to set up. Two minutes a day after that.

The intake copies what the original IEP Companion app asked, as a conversation.

Tell it about your child

First name, year level, and paste in your existing IEP or assessment if you have one. Skip anything you don't. Nothing is ever invented.

Review the plan together

The ally drafts the about-me picture, goals and support team. You correct it before anything is saved. Your framing wins.

Walk in organised

Before any meeting, ask for a brief: your points, your questions, the goals you are holding to, on one printable page.

The team

Nine skills that hand over to each other, plus crisis rails that stop everything when a day gets hard.

Your child's plan is yours

  • The plan folder lives on your computer. Not on a server, not in an account, not with us.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere unless you name a person and say yes, in the moment, every time.
  • Run it on a local model and the whole loop stays offline.
  • Export any time: a printable page, a plain JSON file, a folder you can move to a new laptop.
  • We use a cookieless page counter on this site (OneDollarStats); it sets no cookies and cannot identify you.
